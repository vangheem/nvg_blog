AI is changing how we interact with the workplace, society and world in obvious and non-obvious ways we should pay more attention to.

Let's think about how communication has changed over the ages. We've gone through something like:

- Verbal
- Hand writing on some sort of substrate, limited literacy
- Scribes
- Printing the world
- Mass literacy
- Telegraph
- Telephone
- Email
- Text messages
- Social media


And, as an overlay on those, we have agents that communicate with us and each other.

# Abstractions

What do I mean by abstraction here? Well, an abstraction is a way of providing a different interface to something in order to lower the overhead or cost of using it. It might be that the abstraction is more rich of an experience, or it could provide a more optimized version of what it is abstracting.

In our example of communication, writing things down was a better abstraction than having to memorize things because you might just forget things. Or perhaps, over long distances, the message needed to survive any possible issues with the carrier, etc. Printing press made it so you no longer needed costly scribal work. The cost of producing text for the masses dropped so low that it made sense for common people to learn to read now. And so on, emails and texting made communicating instantaneous with all it's obvious benefits for business.

All these are examples of different abstractions over the fundamental objective: communication between humans.

You might think that "abstractions" might just be a fancier word for technology but I would suggest that there are other forms of abstractions that do not fall under the technology space. Moreover, I would say that some of our abstractions are "leaky" or cause spillage into other abstractions. In software engineering we might could these "leaky abstractions" or maybe, we abstraction debt because different kinds of abstractions have unexpected consequences in other areas of society, life, etc.


# Leaky abstraction: social media

Let us consider the abstraction of social media. We might think that social media is essentially an abstraction on communication with others but let's see how it has spilled over into other realms:

- Friendships
- Advertising
- Political organizing
- Clout or social standing
- Entertainment
- Attention stealing
- Video sharing
- etc

Something that maybe started as a way for you and your friends to communicate and discuss ideas has turned into a massive huge for industry. So much of society, politics and commerce goes through the social media machine.

Maybe even more odd about the whole phenomenon is that social media, with it's ways of to manage "friends" and communicating with others actually seems to have produced a sort of "anti-social" ecosystem. People who use social media are more lonely and anger than ever. Maybe you went online only to do some amazon shopping but soon, someone from the other side of the world has caused you to now be angry about something you had no idea you should be angry about.

It's quite a leaky, terrible abstraction for communicating or making friends. It has certainly become a good means of commerce and attention stealing though. Maybe what is worst about it seems to be how good it is at hijacking our brains to make it addicting so this loneliness and anger inducing machine also gets us to keep coming back to it.


# Other types of abstractions

Abstractions are not only through things like technologies though. For example, math gives us an abstraction through numbers, quantities, structures, patterns, etc that help us to explain and interact with the world. While digital technology based abstractions seem to often be leaky, math seems to be a non-leaky technology. It is self-contained. It's understandings and benefits seem to benefit other realms but not in a coupled and harmful way.

Religion is a very high form of abstraction. It give people a way to understand their existence in relationship to others, time, space, reality and the world. I may write another article on how Christianity, through Christ, gives us the ultimate "abstraction" or correct way to interact and understand existence and the world.


# The Agent abstraction

So, now we come to LLMs and agents. So we've had all this digital communication over the decades that LLMs have trained on and now we have machines that can interact and mimic humans extremely well and at speeds humans can't fathom.

Agents and LLMs short circuit all other digital abstractions. Since they can automate and mimic all human digital things, they enable all parts of the digital age to be managed in a new way.

Essentially, agents give us a single abstraction or interface over the top of potentially all parts of the digital world.

It will be the ultimate or final digital abstraction.


# Are Agents a leaky abstraction?

Sloppy? Yes. Leaky, Probably.

Part of the problem with Agent is how easy it is to generic textual things with them makes it so that humans can keep up.

Because of this, they end up doing way more than the users of the systems might intend and produce so many things that it ends up being wasteful to consume it. The marginal cost of producing so many things is near zero so it just keeps producing. But who is going to consume? If it's humans, it's quite frustrating to have to review a bunch of machine generated slop. If it's for another agent, why?

Now, when Agents are used for communicating with other systems, it is actually an amazingly dynamic abstraction. Having to write interoperable systems used to be a big part of software engineering. The age of Agents has mostly solved that trouble for us.

So a very good abstraction for the internet and other software systems but often a leaky abstraction for humans to consume.

# Is AI an escape hatch to maintain the internet age?

As we saw with the leaky abstraction of social media and how it has had negative side effects on the world and society, are agents doomed to do the same thing to us?

I think there are a couple different ways to go and I'll give some potential options:

1) Internet wasteland: Agents slopify everything on the internet. Most interactions online because agents to make you more angry, get you more addicted and get more effective at advertising. No media online is created by humans anymore. No information can be trusted anymore.
2) Agents short-circuit the whole internet age. Instead of dealing with the old, broken internet age of things, we now utilize the new agentic abstraction to our advantage. It is star-trek like. We have machines that understand us, do things on the old, broken internet for us so we don't have to deal with it. We spend more time with humans, all less anxious, lonely, etc.

Obviously, I prefer #2.

How do we make it happen?
